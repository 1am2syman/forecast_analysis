A forecast is a promise.
The actuals are the receipt.

Every month a forecast is written for a future month. Months later, the real secondary sales land. This notebook is the machinery that lines the two up — product by product, month by month — and measures the gap. Below is the whole pipeline, stage by stage.

03Match forecast to reality
fc.join(clean_actual(), on=['parent_code', 'snop_month'], how='left') .filter(~pl.col('actual_kl').is_null())

Each forecast row is joined to the actuals for the same product and month, then trimmed to only the months that have already happened — the rest is still promises.

fc + actuals → matched rows
04Pick the earliest forecast
.sort(['parent_code','snop_month','calculation_month']) .with_columns( oldest_fc = pl.col('fc_kl').first() .over(['parent_code', 'snop_month']))

Many forecasts can target the same month. Sorted by when they were made and taking .first(), oldest_fc becomes the original promise for that month — the baseline to judge accuracy against.

many fc per month → oldest_fc
05Measure the gap
.with_columns(deficit = oldest_fc - actual_kl) .with_columns(under_ach_pct = deficit / oldest_fc * 100)

The two numbers the whole pipeline exists to produce. deficit in kilolitres; under_ach_pct as a share of the original promise.

oldest_fc · actual_kl → deficit · under_ach_pct

02 / METRICS

The three numbers this produces

Everything upstream exists to compute these. The meters show how to read each value — not live data.

oldest_fc

fc_kl · first() over (parent_code, snop_month)

The earliest forecast made for a month, in kilolitres. The promise everything is measured against.

deficit

oldest_fc − actual_kl

  1. positive → under-sold (promised more than delivered)
  2. negative → over-sold

under_ach_pct

deficit / oldest_fc × 100

  1. 0% — on target
  2. ~50% — watch
  3. 100%+ — nothing delivered
03 / VOCAB

The domain vocabulary

These column names carry the planning world the notebook lives in. SNOP = Sales & Operations Planning.

snop_month
The month being planned — the horizon a forecast aims at.
calculation_month
The month a forecast was produced. One snop_month can have many.
fc_kl
Forecast volume, in kilolitres.
actual_kl
Real secondary sales volume (bill-wise), in kilolitres.
parent_code
The product / SKU grouping key used to join the three sources.
brand
Material group from the product hierarchy — the rollup dimension.
