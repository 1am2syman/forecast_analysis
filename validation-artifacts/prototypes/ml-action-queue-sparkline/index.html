What changes in the UI

Deduplicate by SKU, roll its harmful month rows into one queue item, and plot month-level error improvement in chronological order. Hovering a point can reveal the exact month and evidence.

What does not change

The queue remains filterable and sortable. Priority and total added error are aggregated for the SKU. The CSV keeps the detailed SKU × month evidence rows.
